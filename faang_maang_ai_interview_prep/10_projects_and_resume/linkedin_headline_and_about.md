# LinkedIn Headline & About Template

> **Type:** Template — copy or fill in directly

## Headline (120 char limit)

Formula: `<Role> | <2-3 strongest stack items> | <one differentiator>`

**Example — replace with your own stack/differentiator:**
> Backend Engineer | Django, DRF, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch | Building high-throughput document
> processing systems

Avoid: "Passionate software engineer" / "Tech enthusiast" — says nothing a recruiter can search
or filter on. Recruiters keyword-search headlines; put your real stack in it.

## About section structure

```markdown
<2-3 sentences: who you are, years of experience, core stack, what kind of problems you like
solving>

<1 short paragraph: your strongest project or impact, one concrete number if you have one>

<1 short paragraph: what you're looking for / open to, if actively job-searching — optional,
skip if not>

<Optional: links to GitHub, portfolio, or a blog post you've written>
```

**Example — replace with your own facts:**
> Backend engineer with 3 years building production APIs in Django/DRF, backed by PostgreSQL and
> Elasticsearch, deployed with Docker across microservices. I like problems where correctness and
> latency both matter — search ranking, ingestion pipelines, and OCR/document processing systems.
>
> Most recently redesigned a search API's pagination layer, cutting p95 latency by over 80% for a
> ~40k DAU product. Increasingly interested in the backend systems that support LLM applications
> — retrieval, embeddings, and serving infrastructure.
>
> Open to Backend Engineer / AI Backend Engineer roles at product-focused companies.

## Rules

- Every sentence should contain a real noun (a technology, a number, a domain) — delete filler
  adjectives ("passionate," "results-driven," "dynamic").
- Keep it under ~150 words. Recruiters skim.
- Update it whenever your resume changes — stale mismatched profiles cost credibility.
