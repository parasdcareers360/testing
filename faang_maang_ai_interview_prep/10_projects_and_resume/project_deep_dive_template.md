# Project Deep-Dive Prep Sheet

> **Type:** Template — copy or fill in directly

Fill one of these per project **before** any onsite that lists a "project deep dive" or
"resume-based" round — most FAANG/MAANG loops have at least one.

## Prep sheet

```markdown
### Project: <name>

**60-second pitch:** (memorize this cold)
<see project_architecture_explanation_template.md section 1-2>

**Architecture diagram:** (able to draw from memory)
[ ] Practiced drawing without notes

**3 technical decisions and their trade-offs:**
1. <decision> — chose X over Y because ___, trade-off was ___
2. <decision>
3. <decision>

**Hardest bug/issue and how you found it:**
<specific, with the debugging process, not just the fix>

**What you'd change with more time/at 10x scale:**
<shows seniority>

**Numbers you can defend on the spot:**
| Metric | Value | How measured |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

**Anticipated tough questions + your answer:**
| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| "Why not use <alternative tech>?" | |
| "How does this handle <failure mode>?" | |
| "What's the biggest weakness of this design?" | |
```

## Rules

- Do this for your **top 2-3 projects only** — a resume with 6 listed projects doesn't mean
  6 deep-dive-ready stories; pick the strongest.
- Redo the "anticipated tough questions" row after every mock interview or real interview where
  you got caught off guard — that's the fastest way this sheet improves.
- Cross-check every number here against what's actually printed on your resume
  ([`faang_maang_resume_template.md`](faang_maang_resume_template.md)) — inconsistency between
  resume and spoken answer is a credibility red flag.

See [`project_architecture_explanation_template.md`](project_architecture_explanation_template.md)
for the delivery structure and [`12_mock_interviews/`](../12_mock_interviews/) to rehearse this
under real conditions.
