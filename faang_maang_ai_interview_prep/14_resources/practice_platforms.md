# Practice Platforms

> **Type:** Study notes

Where to actually *do* the work tracked in this workspace's trackers.

## Coding practice

- **LeetCode** — the primary platform for problems referenced in every
  `02_dsa_and_coding/patterns/<topic>/problem_tracker.md`; the "LeetCode-style" problems in this
  workspace are named to be searchable there directly.
- **NeetCode 150 / NeetCode All (neetcode.io)** — a curated subset of LeetCode problems organized
  by the same pattern taxonomy used here; useful as a second problem source per pattern once
  you've exhausted the tracker rows.

## Mock interviews

- **Pramp** — free, peer-matched mock interviews (coding and, less commonly, system design);
  good first source referenced in
  [`../00_master_plan/mock_interview_schedule.md`](../00_master_plan/mock_interview_schedule.md).
- **interviewing.io** — anonymous mock interviews with engineers from target companies, paid but
  closer to real-loop conditions; worth it once you're consistently solving Mediums cleanly.
- **Peers / former colleagues** — the cheapest and often highest-signal option for behavioral and
  system design mocks specifically, since a peer who knows your actual work can push on realism.

## System design practice

- **Excalidraw or a plain whiteboard** — practice sketching the Mermaid-style diagrams from
  [`06_system_design/exercises/`](../06_system_design/exercises/) by hand under a timer; most
  onsite/virtual system design rounds expect you to draw, not just talk.

## SQL practice

- **The sandbox referenced in [`../04_sql_and_databases/sql_practice_questions.md`](../04_sql_and_databases/sql_practice_questions.md)**
  and any local PostgreSQL instance — running real queries against real (even toy) data catches
  syntax and index-usage mistakes that reading alone won't.

## Behavioral practice

- **Self-recording (phone camera, timed)** — reviewing your own delivery against
  [`../12_mock_interviews/interview_feedback_rubric.md`](../12_mock_interviews/interview_feedback_rubric.md)
  catches rambling and pacing issues faster than any platform.

## How to use this list

Match platform to what `problem_solving_progress_tracker.md` or `weakness_tracker.md` says you're
behind on — don't rotate platforms for novelty, consistency on one or two matters more.
