# Mock Interview Schedule

> **Type:** Study notes + tracker table

## Why start in week 4, not week 12

Mock interviews reveal *communication* and *composure* gaps that solo practice never surfaces —
these take longer to fix than technical gaps, so they need the most calendar time to iterate on.
Starting late means you find out about a communication problem with no runway left to fix it.

## Cadence

| Week | Mock type | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Coding | Baseline: can you solve + communicate under a stranger's eyes at all? |
| 6 | Coding | Check improvement on week-4 feedback |
| 7 | System design | Baseline for HLD communication and structure |
| 8 | Coding | Push into Medium/Hard territory |
| 9 | Behavioral | Baseline: story delivery, STAR structure, conciseness |
| 10 | Coding or System design (whichever scored lower) | Targeted fix |
| 11 | **Full loop**: coding + system design + behavioral, same week | Stamina + context-switching simulation |
| 12 | One final confidence-building mock (any weak area) | Close out, not to "learn" something new |

That's roughly one mock every 1.5-2 weeks starting week 4, ~7-8 total before any real onsite loop.

## Where to source mocks

- **Peers**: colleagues or friends at target companies — highest realism, often free (reciprocate
  with mocks for them).
- **Structured peer-mock platforms**: pairing with another candidate prepping for the same
  companies (many communities/Discords organize this) — free, but interviewer quality varies.
- **Paid mock services / coaches**: highest consistency of feedback quality, worth it for at least
  1-2 of your system design and behavioral mocks specifically, since self-assessment on those two
  is harder than for coding (where a test suite tells you if you're right).
- **Self-mock**: solo, out loud, recorded — better than nothing, use for the *weeks between*
  scheduled mocks with another person, not as a substitute for all of them. Recording yourself and
  watching it back is uncomfortable and extremely effective.

## Every mock, regardless of source

1. Use the matching template: `12_mock_interviews/coding_mock_interview_template.md`,
   `system_design_mock_interview_template.md`, or `behavioral_mock_interview_template.md`.
2. Fill `12_mock_interviews/interview_feedback_rubric.md` immediately after (or have your mock
   interviewer fill it) — don't rely on memory a day later.
3. Push every concrete gap into `12_mock_interviews/weakness_tracker.md` with a specific next
   action, not just a score.
4. Any recurring technical mistake also goes into `mistake_log.md`.
