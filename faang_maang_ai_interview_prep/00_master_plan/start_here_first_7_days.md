# Start Here — Your First 7 Days

> **Type:** Template — follow it, check items off, then move to `12_week_plan.md` Week 2

Don't open every folder in this workspace on day 1. Do these things, in order, over your first
week, then let the 12-week plan take over.

## Day 1
- [ ] Read `README.md` (root) and this file fully.
- [ ] Read `priority_topics.md` so you understand *why* the plan is shaped the way it is.
- [ ] Read `12_week_plan.md` once, fully, without trying to memorize it.
- [ ] Pick your fixed weekday study time (e.g. 7:30-9:30pm) and weekend blocks. Put them in your
      actual calendar right now — this is the single highest-leverage thing you'll do all week.

## Day 2
- [ ] Skim `01_company_interview_patterns/README.md` and the docs for your top 2-3 target companies.
- [ ] Read `02_dsa_and_coding/coding_interview_framework.md` — the 7-step communication framework
      you'll use in every coding interview from now on. Memorize the 7 steps, not the wording.
- [ ] Do your first problem: `02_dsa_and_coding/patterns/01_arrays_and_hashing/concept.md`, pick
      an Easy from its `problem_tracker.md`, write your solution in `solutions/`.

## Day 3
- [ ] Read `03_python_core_and_advanced/01_data_model_and_object_references.md` and
      `02_mutable_vs_immutable.md`.
- [ ] Second DSA problem from Arrays & Hashing (Medium this time).
- [ ] Start your resume: copy `10_projects_and_resume/faang_maang_resume_template.md` into your own
      file (outside this repo or in a personal fork) and fill in your real experience.

## Day 4
- [ ] Read `03_python_core_and_advanced/03_shallow_vs_deep_copy.md`.
- [ ] Third DSA problem (Two Pointers pattern, `patterns/02_two_pointers/`).
- [ ] Read `11_behavioral_and_leadership/star_method_guide.md`; draft your first STAR story into
      `story_bank_tracker.md` (pick your strongest, most recent project story).

## Day 5
- [ ] DSA: one more Two Pointers problem.
- [ ] Read `05_backend_engineering/01_rest_api_design.md` — closest to your actual day job, should
      feel familiar and confidence-building.
- [ ] Draft a second STAR story.

## Day 6 (weekend block 1)
- [ ] Longer DSA session: 3 problems mixing Arrays/Hashing and Two Pointers.
- [ ] Read `06_system_design/fundamentals/requirement_clarification.md` and
      `capacity_estimation.md`.
- [ ] Fill in `10_projects_and_resume/project_deep_dive_template.md` for your single best production
      project — this becomes your go-to system-design-adjacent behavioral answer.

## Day 7 (weekend block 2)
- [ ] Fill out `00_master_plan/weekly_review.md` for week 1 — be honest about what you didn't get to.
- [ ] Fill out `15_progress_tracking/skills_self_assessment.md` as your baseline (you'll compare
      against this at week 6 and week 12).
- [ ] Read `12_week_plan.md` Week 2 section and adjust your calendar blocks for week 2 if week 1's
      schedule didn't actually hold up in practice.
- [ ] Draft a third STAR story so you start week 2 with 3 in the bank.

## What "done" looks like after 7 days

- [ ] Fixed weekday/weekend study blocks on your calendar
- [ ] 5+ DSA problems solved and logged in trackers
- [ ] Resume v1 drafted
- [ ] 3 STAR stories drafted
- [ ] 1 project deep-dive filled in
- [ ] Week 1 review completed, baseline skills self-assessment done

If you're behind on this list by day 7, that's normal — trim scope in `12_week_plan.md` Week 2
rather than trying to cram day 1-7 and week 2 together.
