# Priority Topics — What Matters Most for This Profile

> **Type:** Study notes

You have ~220-240 hours across 12 weeks. You cannot max out every folder in this workspace to the
same depth. This is the ranked priority order and the reasoning behind it, so time-boxing decisions
are easy later.

## Tier 1 — non-negotiable, most interview weight (spend the most hours here)

1. **DSA patterns (`02_dsa_and_coding/`)** — every loop at every one of these companies has 2-5
   coding rounds. This is the single highest-leverage folder. At 3 YOE you're expected to be fast
   and clean on Medium, competent on Hard.
2. **System design (`06_system_design/`)** — at 3 YOE you'll usually get *one* system design round
   (sometimes none at the lowest levels, sometimes it's folded into a "practical" round at
   startups/AI labs). Still Tier 1 because it's often the differentiator round and the one people
   under-prepare for relative to DSA.
3. **Python core (`03_python_core_and_advanced/`)** — you will get language-depth questions,
   especially at companies that don't do heavy LeetCode-style loops (AI labs, some startups), and
   Python fluency shows up implicitly in every coding round's code quality signal.

## Tier 2 — high weight, especially for *your* background and target roles

4. **Backend engineering (`05_backend_engineering/`)** — this is where your 3 years of real Django/
   DRF/Docker/microservices experience becomes your strongest differentiator in behavioral and
   "tell me about a system you built" conversations. Underrated by candidates who only grind LeetCode.
5. **SQL and databases (`04_sql_and_databases/`)** — near-universal for backend roles; some
   companies (Meta, many product-data-heavy companies) run dedicated SQL rounds.
6. **Low-level design (`07_low_level_design/`)** — common at Amazon-style loops and mid-size/AI
   companies more than at Google/Meta, but cheap to prepare (SOLID + a handful of exercises) and
   high win-rate when it does show up.
9. **Behavioral (`11_behavioral_and_leadership/`)** — every single company has this round, and
   people fail it more often from *lack of prepared stories* than lack of substance. Cheap to fix.

## Tier 3 — differentiator for AI-company targets specifically

7. **AI backend & LLM systems (`09_ai_backend_and_llm_systems/`)** — if OpenAI/Anthropic/DeepMind/
   Microsoft AI/AI-infra roles are a real target, this tier becomes Tier 1 for you, not Tier 3.
   Calibrate based on how much of your application list is AI-company vs. classic FAANG.
8. **AI/ML/NLP foundations (`08_ai_ml_nlp/`)** — supports #7; you don't need to be an ML researcher,
   you need enough fluency to not get lost in a conversation about embeddings, evaluation, or
   training vs. inference trade-offs.

## Tier 4 — important but not where hours go first

10. **Company-specific patterns (`01_company_interview_patterns/`)** — read once per company you're
    actually applying to, right before you apply, not as ongoing study time.
11. **Resume/projects/storytelling (`10_projects_and_resume/`)** — front-load this in week 1-2 (you
    need a resume to start applying by week 6-7), then it's mostly done.
12. **Mock interviews (`12_mock_interviews/`)** — not a "topic" but a *cadence*; see
    `mock_interview_schedule.md`. Time-boxed, not open-ended study time.

## Suggested rough hour split across 12 weeks (~230 hours)

| Area | Hours | % |
|---|---|---|
| DSA patterns | 75 | 33% |
| System design (HLD) | 30 | 13% |
| Python core | 25 | 11% |
| Backend engineering | 25 | 11% |
| SQL & databases | 15 | 7% |
| Low-level design | 12 | 5% |
| Behavioral prep | 12 | 5% |
| AI/ML/NLP + AI backend (adjust up if AI-focused) | 20 | 9% |
| Resume/projects/company research | 10 | 4% |
| Mock interviews (on top of the above, overlapping weeks 5-12) | ~15 sessions, ~15-20h | — |

If AI companies are your primary target, shift ~15-20 hours from DSA/system-design into
`08_ai_ml_nlp/` and `09_ai_backend_and_llm_systems/` — those companies still bar-raise hard on
coding and system design, they just also expect you to be conversational on LLM systems.
