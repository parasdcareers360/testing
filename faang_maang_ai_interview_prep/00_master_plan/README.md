## The core assumption

You are working full-time. This plan assumes:
- ~2 hours/weekday (evenings), ~5-6 hours/day on weekends (split across two sessions) = **~18-20
  hours/week**.
- 12 weeks = **~220-240 hours total**, which is enough to get interview-ready for a 3-YOE backend
  role, *not* enough to become an expert in everything. The plan is deliberately ruthless about
  priority order — see `priority_topics.md`.
- You will apply to companies starting around **week 6-7**, not after week 12 — interview loops
  take 3-6 weeks to schedule end-to-end, so applications need a head start on your peak readiness.
