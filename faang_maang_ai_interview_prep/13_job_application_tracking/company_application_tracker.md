## Notes

- Log the application the same day, not "later" — pipelines move fast and gaps in this table cost
  you tracked follow-up dates.
- On rejection, spend 2 minutes asking *why* (recruiter feedback if offered, or your own honest
  read of which round was weakest) before moving on — that's a data point for
  [`weekly_scorecard.md`](weekly_scorecard.md).
