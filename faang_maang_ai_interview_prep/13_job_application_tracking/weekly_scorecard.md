# Weekly Scorecard

> **Type:** Template — copy or fill in directly

One line per week across both prep and job-search axes — the fastest way to see if effort is
translating into pipeline movement. Fill this in alongside
[`../00_master_plan/weekly_review.md`](../00_master_plan/weekly_review.md).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Reading the trend

If applications sent stays high but phone screens stay at zero for 3+ weeks, the problem is
upstream of interviewing — check resume/targeting first (see
[`../10_projects_and_resume/`](../10_projects_and_resume/)), not your DSA pace. If phone screens
convert but technical rounds don't, that's a signal to increase mock frequency (see
[`../00_master_plan/mock_interview_schedule.md`](../00_master_plan/mock_interview_schedule.md)).
