> STAR-based "bar raiser" format are well-established and consistent across Amazon.

## Why this matters specifically for Amazon

Amazon is unusually explicit: interviewers are assigned specific Leadership Principles to probe,
and a "bar raiser" on the panel is specifically evaluating LP fit independent of the hiring
manager. Having at least one solid story per LP — not just per question type — is the single
highest-leverage prep activity for an Amazon loop.

## Reuse, don't multiply

You don't need 16 different stories — most candidates cover all 16 with **6-8 real stories**,
each mapped to 2-3 LPs depending on which part you emphasize. Example: a story about redesigning
a slow query can hit **Dive Deep** (root-causing with EXPLAIN ANALYZE), **Ownership** (you found
it and fixed it without being told), and **Deliver Results** (the measurable latency win) — same
story, three angles depending on the question asked.

## "Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit" — the one candidates most often get wrong

This LP wants a story where you **disagreed, made your case, lost the argument, and then
genuinely committed** to the team's decision (not silently resented it or sabotaged it). A story
where you disagreed and were proven right isn't the point of this LP — commitment after losing
is. Keep a story specifically for this shape, separate from `conflict_resolution_questions.md`'s
broader disagreement stories.
