start of the plan, then again every 3-4 weeks. The point isn't the absolute number — it's the
*delta* and which areas aren't moving.

## Why self-rate instead of just trusting mock feedback

Mock feedback is sparse (a handful of data points); this is a continuous signal you control the
cadence of. Where your self-rating and mock feedback disagree by 2+ points, trust the mock feedback
— that gap is usually the blind spot itself.
