## Why a separate log from `weekly_review.md`

`weekly_review.md` captures *why* and *how it felt* in prose. This log is the compressed version —
scan the "On track?" column top to bottom to see the whole arc's health in 10 seconds, then open
the matching `weekly_review.md` entry only for weeks that need explaining.
