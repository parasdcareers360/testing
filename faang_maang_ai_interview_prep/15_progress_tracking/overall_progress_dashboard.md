## Where each column comes from

- DSA problems / patterns covered → `13_job_application_tracking/problem_solving_progress_tracker.md`
- System design / LLD exercises done → tick counts from
  [`../06_system_design/exercises/`](../06_system_design/exercises/) and
  [`../07_low_level_design/exercises/`](../07_low_level_design/exercises/)
- Mock interviews done → count of filled-in templates in
  [`../12_mock_interviews/`](../12_mock_interviews/)
- Applications sent → `13_job_application_tracking/company_application_tracker.md`
- Stories in story bank → `../11_behavioral_and_leadership/story_bank_tracker.md`

## Reading it

A flat or declining "patterns fully covered" line while "DSA problems" keeps climbing means you're
spreading thin instead of finishing patterns — check `priority_topics.md` and consider narrowing
focus for a week.
